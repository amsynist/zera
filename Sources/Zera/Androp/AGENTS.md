# Android sharing in Zera

AndroidTransfer owns the in-process AndropKit receiver. The menu enables/disables it and exposes pairing. Reuse the previous com.androp.mac pairing code when valid, so paired phones stay paired. The package is a source snapshot, not a helper process. Keep port 8989 and Bonjour identity compatible with the Android app. The existing IslandView itself morphs open; reuse its companion catch/success gestures, palette and controls. All details render below the hardware notch. No separate receipt panel. The swipe card follows the phone drag from the screen edge and, on release, is pulled into the notch and vanishes (AndropKit `swallowsAtLanding`); then the island's stream pours. No card stays on the desktop. The island opens as the swipe begins; its own edge (sides and bottom, fading toward the notch) glows in the theme accent, breathing during the swipe and beating with the tide while bytes arrive. Phone transfers send no light outside the island. Zera leaps to catch the card on release. Photos and videos travel as their own picture (the phone's preview), on the card and in the receipt. Preserve existing CardKind raw IDs. Delayed cleanup must not hide a different screen. Add to Shelf only after the receiver verifies and saves the original. No automatic analysis. Photos, videos and plain text fall onto the screen and open after the verified save (autoOpenReceivedMedia); other files drop into the notch and only reach the Shelf. Photos and videos skip the notch panel: they land on the desktop and open; the panel appears only if their transfer is still running after 1.2 s or fails, and closes with the card as the file opens. Gallery (system) drags only reveal the picture at the drop, so a picture-shaped pane follows the live split until then. On cancel/disable/disconnect, remove stale UI; ID guards prevent old callbacks hiding newer transfers. Preserve unrelated in-progress Zera work. Host tests: `swift test --filter AndroidTransferTests`. See docs/ANDROID_SHARING.md.
