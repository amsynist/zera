INSTALL ZERA

Works on macOS 13 or newer, with Apple silicon or Intel.
You do not need Terminal, Xcode, a Zera account or Claude Code to install.

1. Download the DMG from https://zera.pravincodes.in/
   Official releases: https://github.com/amsynist/zera/releases/latest
2. Open Zera-<version>.dmg. Drag Zera onto the Applications shortcut.
3. In Finder, open Applications and double-click Zera.
4. Eject the Zera disk image in Finder. You can delete the downloaded DMG.

Zera lives at /Applications/Zera.app. She appears at the top of your screen,
under the notch or in the middle of the menu bar on Macs without a notch.
Hover Zera to open her menu. Connect optional integrations in Settings later.

IF MACOS BLOCKS THE FIRST LAUNCH

For a release that has not been notarized:
1. Try opening Zera from Applications first.
2. Open System Settings > Privacy & Security and scroll to Security.
3. Choose Open Anyway beside Zera. Authenticate if asked, then confirm Open.
macOS remembers the exception for that copy.

Only approve a copy you downloaded from the official site or repository.
Apple's guide: https://support.apple.com/en-us/102445

OPTIONAL TERMINAL METHOD

After moving the official download to Applications, open Terminal and paste:

xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine "/Applications/Zera.app"
open "/Applications/Zera.app"

This removes quarantine from Zera only. It does not sign or notarize the app.
If macOS reports malware or a damaged app, download a fresh copy and report
the issue instead of bypassing that warning. On a managed Mac, ask your IT
administrator if installation is restricted.

UPDATING

Quit Zera using its own menu, download the new DMG, and drag the new Zera
into Applications. Choose Replace when Finder asks, then reopen Zera.
Your settings stay on your Mac. A new unnotarized copy may need approval.

UNINSTALLING

In Settings > Integrations, remove any installed hooks and stop usage
tracking first. Turn off Open at login in Settings > General, quit Zera,
and move /Applications/Zera.app to Trash.

Help: https://github.com/amsynist/zera/issues
