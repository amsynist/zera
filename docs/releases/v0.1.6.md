## A little more personality

Zera 0.1.6 brings live character animation and playful water breaks to the notch.

## What's new

- **Live Zera:** glossy eyes follow your cursor, natural blinks and breathing keep her moving, and hover and taps get a response. Four quick taps earn a playful huff.
- **Shared screen animation:** the same animation clock and interactions power the hanging buddy across the app's screens. The app opener keeps its existing design.
- **Water breaks with company:** Zera crouches, jumps over to your cursor and lands softly with her boba. She taps, speaks and cycles through six funny waiting expressions until you confirm a sip.
- **Playful replies:** **Took a sip!** earns a happy cheer and sends her back to her perch. **One sec…** gives you 20 quiet seconds without completing the reminder. Hover the sip button and she smiles.
- **Website companion:** a live hanging Zera and an interactive water-break preview, with desktop and mobile layouts. The README explains how to set up hydration reminders.

## Refinements

- Extra transparent animation space keeps the water buddy visible while leaning, bouncing and squashing after landing, without shrinking the character.
- Hidden or occluded app mascots pause their shared clock; the website water preview pauses offscreen and in hidden tabs.
- Reduce Motion skips the water flights and body animation. Visits do not steal focus or send clicks or keyboard shortcuts to other apps.
- The temporary one-minute hydration reminder used during development was removed. Reminder intervals remain user-configurable.

## Validation

- Full local suite passed: 228 tests, 17 optional skips, no failures.
- Animation render previews, pixel-edge clipping checks, website interactions and the universal release build passed.

The release workflow reruns the test suite and builds the download packages for this tag. Website deployment is separate and runs after the website changes reach `main`.
