- New default opener look, the launcher: `⌥ Space` raises a card from the bottom of the screen with your apps above a search field. Type `>` for commands (Wallpaper, Theme, Lock, Sleep, Quit All Apps, Kill Process, Kill Port and Zera’s own actions). Orbit, Tree and Minimal remain in Settings › General › Opener style. The flow is inspired by Caelestia shell; none of its code is used.
- `>wallpaper` shows pictures as a strip, previews the chosen one behind your windows as you move with ← →, and changes the real desktop only on Return. `>folders` (or Settings › General) adds the folders it reads; with none added it uses `~/Pictures/Wallpapers`, then the pictures that come with macOS.
- Carrying any file, picture or video up to the notch now opens the Shelf to catch it, with a lit outline that closes on the drop. Files promised by Photos, Mail and browsers are accepted, and a quick pickup is no longer missed.
- Settings are regrouped under labelled sections. A new Features pane holds tasks and focus, break reminders, battery alerts, voice, music, weather and the teleprompter; General and Notch keep only what belongs to them.
- The website’s opener section shows all four looks live behind a switcher.
