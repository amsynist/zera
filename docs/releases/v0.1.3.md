## Tasks, focus and timesheets — right from your notch

Zera 0.1.3 adds a workspace for planning your day, staying focused and turning completed work into a timesheet, plus faster access to your favorite apps.

## What's new

- **Tasks and projects:** a new Tasks tab with Today, Week and Month views, project filters and completed work grouped by day. Add a task with natural shorthand such as `Write docs #project 30m`, and rename projects or move tasks between them.
- **Focus orb:** start a task to bring up a timer at the screen edge. Open the focus card to pause, finish or move to the next task; hover the orb for quick add, or use **⌥⌘T** from anywhere. The timer pauses automatically after five minutes away.
- **Timesheets from commits:** link a project to a repository or a folder of repositories. Your commits become completed tasks, grouped by Claude Code or listed individually when grouping is unavailable. Rebuild recent history and see which repositories contributed to each task. Commit-based time totals are estimates.
- **Copy and export your work:** copy a day's tasks with one click, include times and repository names when needed, or export to **Excel, CSV or dated text**. Timesheets can round time to quarter hours.
- **Pinned and recent apps:** pin or unpin an app with **⇧⌘P** and reorder pins with **⌥⌘↑ / ↓**. Your usual puts pinned apps first, followed by recent and running apps, making it easier to switch back to what you were using.

## Refinements

- Context menus now match Zera's visual style.
- App-opener buttons hide shortcut hints when space is tight, keeping labels from overflowing.
- Refreshed screenshots, a complete recorded app tour and a new landing page published through GitHub Pages.
