## Daily Shelf

Save links and notes to a shelf you can place at the edge of your Mac. GitHub pull requests and other known links are filed automatically. Connect GitHub to see checks and reviews for the PRs you save. Pin items to keep them for the next day or reopen a past shelf from history.

Daily Shelf is available under **Settings → Shelf**. Optional Claude sorting sends link URLs and titles. Your notes stay local. [Watch the Daily Shelf film](https://zera.pravincodes.in/#daily-shelf)

## Signal and Agents

The Signal interface brings shared typography, controls and themed surfaces to the notch. The Agents screen shows Claude and Codex together, including replies and approvals for managed sessions and read-only activity from observed desktop chats. [Explore the app](https://zera.pravincodes.in/#screens)

## Android sharing

Send original photos and documents from Androp to Zera over local Wi-Fi or a trusted phone hotspot. Photos and videos can open after a verified save. Other files appear on the file Shelf. [Pairing guide](https://github.com/amsynist/zera/blob/v2.1.0/docs/ANDROID_SHARING.md) · [Watch the sharing film](https://zera.pravincodes.in/#sharing-film)
