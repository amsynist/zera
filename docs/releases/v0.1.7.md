## Splash

Zera 0.1.7 turns the water reminder into Splash: a glass of water pops up beside your cursor and Zera jumps into it.

## What's new

- **Splash water breaks:** when it's time, a clear glass of water appears just beside your cursor (never under it, so clicks still land). Zera lets go of her rope, flips through the air from the notch and lands in the glass with a splash, then waves from the water.
- **Two answers:** a slim glass bar beside her shows **Water break** with **Drank** and **10 min**. Drank: the water goes down as she drinks, then she jumps back up to her rope and the reminder is done. 10 min: she jumps home and the reminder comes back in ten minutes.
- **No nagging:** left alone for 30 seconds, she heads home and leaves a small drop under the notch. Click it when you're ready and she comes back down. In full-screen apps, or when Zera is hidden, only the drop appears.
- **Today's water:** the bar shows how many glasses you've had against the water reminders scheduled for today, like “4 of 8 today”. After each sip a tiny glass under the notch shows the count for a few seconds, then tucks away.
- **Your week, as a card:** click that glass, or choose **Water this week…** from the Zera menu-bar icon, for your last seven days as seven glasses with your total and streak. **Save image** puts a 1080 × 1350 card in Downloads, ready to share. On Fridays, or the day you reach a 7-day streak, Zera mentions it once.
- **A Drank button made of water:** a little water sloshes in it at rest, rises when you point at it, and a tap fills it to the brim with waves and bubbles.
- **Tiled hover menu** and refined speech: Zera's captions get their comic pointers back, with smoother water surfaces and arc shadows.

## Refinements

- Glasses are counted from the reminders' existing history on your Mac (60 days); nothing new is stored or sent anywhere.
- Only the visit's two buttons take clicks; the rest of its area lets clicks through. It never takes focus.
- Reduce Motion skips the jump, the splash and the water animation.
- The README and the website's water preview show Splash, including the brief glass counter.

## Validation

- Full local suite passed: 237 tests, 17 optional skips, no failures.
- Water visit renders (jump, splash, waiting, drinking, return), the weekly card at 1080 × 1350, and the website demo in a browser.

The release workflow reruns the test suite and builds the download packages for this tag. Website deployment is separate and runs after the website changes reach `main`.
