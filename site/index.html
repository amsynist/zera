A few useful details
Does sharing need internet?

No. Phone and Mac need the same local Wi-Fi or a phone-hotspot connection. There is no cloud relay. Bluetooth and automatic physical device positioning are not implemented.

Can I drag out of any Android app?

The background overlay can receive global drags that the source app exports. Support depends on the app and Android/One UI mode. Androp’s own gallery, Files tab and Share → Androp are available alternatives.

What leaves my Mac?

Received originals stay on the local network and save in Downloads/Androp. The transfer is paired but unencrypted. Optional Claude/Codex, GitHub, weather and other integrations have their own data flows and setup. A file is sent for analysis only when you ask. Read the data map.

What if my Mac has no notch?

Zera uses the center of the menu bar. The same screens and tools are available.

Is everything in the video a real screen?

The app surfaces are fresh native captures of Zera and Androp with synthetic artwork and fictional demo data. The device framing and presentation are motion design. Transfer time varies by file and network; the film is not a performance benchmark.
