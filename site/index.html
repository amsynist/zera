02AGENTSclaude and codex, together

See your agents without hunting for the window.

When a session starts working, two wings slide out of the notch with what it is doing. When it needs a decision, the command is right there. Approve it, reject it, or reply when it is done.

  • Claude and Codex in one list. Sessions keep their own colour across the wings, alerts and the Agents screen.
  • Managed sessions take replies and approvals. Observed desktop chats are shown read-only; Zera does not control them.
  • Your tools, your logins. Zera uses the Claude and Codex you already installed.

03DAILY SHELFtoday’s links, at the edge

Where did that PR go? It’s on the shelf.

Paste a pull request, a ticket, a doc or a note. Daily Shelf files the links it recognises and waits at the edge of your screen until you need them.

  • Only what you add. Nothing is read from your browser or history.
  • Checks and reviews for the PRs you saved, once GitHub is connected.
  • Clears at 4:00. Pinned items stay for tomorrow; past shelves are in history.

Try it: type github.com/you/repo/pull/12 or any note, then ↵. Nothing is fetched or stored.

05LAUNCHERapps, commands, wallpapers

A launcher that rises from the bottom edge.

Press ⌥ Space and type to find an app. Type > and the list turns into commands: Wallpaper, Theme, Lock, Sleep, Quit All, Kill Process, Kill Port and Zera’s own actions.

  • >wallpaper is a strip of pictures. Move with ← → and the desktop follows. Nothing is set until you press Return, and the pictures come from folders you choose.
  • Four looks, one shortcut. Launcher is the default. Minimal, Tree and Orbit stay in Settings; try each one here.
  • App Shortcuts are optional and need no Accessibility or Screen Recording permission.
  • Not a window manager. Zera opens and focuses apps; it never moves your windows.

Try it: type a few letters, or > then wallpaper, a space and ← →. ↵ runs the choice.

The launcher’s flow is inspired by Caelestia shell for Hyprland, rebuilt natively for macOS in Zera’s Signal style. Zera uses none of its code.

07SHARINGandroid to mac · no internet, just wi-fi

From your phone, into your Mac.

Glide a photo off the edge of your phone in Androp and it arrives on the Mac as the original. Documents drop into the notch, where a liquid receipt fills as the bytes arrive, then wait on the Shelf.

  • Local only. Same Wi-Fi or your phone’s hotspot. No cloud relay.
  • Verified originals. A file counts as saved only after its SHA-256 checks out.
  • Paired, not encrypted. Use a network you trust.
Androp for Android ↓ Pairing guide ↗

  1. Same local network

    Wi-Fi at home, or your phone’s hotspot outside.

  2. Pair with the code

    Turn on receiving under Extras → Android sharing, then enter the code in Androp.

  3. Glide it over

    From Androp’s gallery or Files tab, or Share → Androp in another app.

08PRIVACYlocal first, and plain about the rest

What stays, and what leaves.

Zera has no telemetry service and no account. Integrations are opt-in, and each one is listed in the privacy data map.

ON THIS MAC

Clipboard history, tasks, timesheets, notes and shelf references.

IN KEYCHAIN

Integration tokens. Nothing is written to plain files.

ONLY WHEN YOU ASK

A file is sent to your chosen provider when you ask about it. Optional link sorting sends URLs and titles only.

ON DEVICE

Dictation with Murmur runs on a local Whisper model after a one-time download.

QUESTIONS

What if my Mac has no notch?

Zera uses the centre of the menu bar. The same screens and tools are there.

Does Zera control my Claude or Codex sessions?

Managed sessions that Zera starts accept replies and approvals. Chats it only observes on the desktop are read-only. It uses the tools and logins you already have.

Is the desktop on this page the real app?

No. It is a browser recreation with fictional data so you can try the feel of it. The native capture and the films show the real app, also with sample data.

Does Android sharing need internet?

No. Phone and Mac need the same local Wi-Fi or a phone hotspot. There is no cloud relay and no Bluetooth transport. Transfers are paired and unencrypted, so use a network you trust.

How do I pair Android?

Enable receiving in Zera’s Extras, choose Show pairing QR, then tap Scan pairing QR in Zera X. Older APKs can enter the Mac’s local IP address and pairing code manually. Claude approvals use a separate QR under Zera X approvals.
